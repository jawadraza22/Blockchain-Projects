Platform Features

🔗
On-Chain Storage
Every complaint is permanently stored on the Sepolia blockchain with cryptographic proofs.
🔐
Role-Based Access
8 distinct roles with granular permissions and MetaMask wallet authentication.
📋
Audit Trail
Complete immutable history of every status change, note, and escalation.
👍
Community Voting
Students can vote to support complaints. One vote per wallet, no duplicates.
⚠️
Auto Escalation
Complaints unresolved after 7 days are automatically escalated to Admin.
⭐
Feedback & Rating
Students rate resolution quality 1–5 stars. Stored permanently on-chain.
🕵️
Anonymous Mode
Submit complaints without revealing your wallet identity.
📁
Evidence Upload
Upload images & PDFs to Supabase. SHA-256 hash stored on-chain for integrity.
